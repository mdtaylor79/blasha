About Blasha

Blasha is an AI-created recording artist developed by Purpose Wave Entertainment. Her sound blends feel-good R&B, contemporary soul, melodic hip-hop, and pop to create music that is uplifting, confident, feminine, and emotionally honest.

She represents a young woman who is still growing but already understands the importance of self-awareness, purpose, and identity. Her music explores empowerment, relationships, ambition, self-respect, growth, and authenticity.
